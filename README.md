## Why Apposition?

**Stop setting up your desk every morning.** Standard browsers force you to reopen and arrange tabs daily. Apposition remembers exactly how you laid out a client's Jira, Slack, and Shopify. You click the workspace, and jump into deep work in one second.

**Unify notifications without the clutter.** Managing five Slack accounts and four Gmails in a browser tab bar creates anxiety. Apposition unifies notifications in one place so you catch urgent client messages without needing twenty tabs permanently open.

**Silence your laptop fans.** Running multiple Chrome profiles consumes RAM and drains batteries quickly. Apposition efficiently suspends inactive client workspaces, returning memory to your operating system.

**Prevent catastrophic mistakes.** Apposition uses UI-enforced sandboxing, not just cookie isolation. You cannot accidentally paste one client's sensitive data into another client's dashboard because the sessions are structurally separated.

## Features

- **Tiling Workspace Engine:** Snap web apps into gapless grids or "Master & Stack" layouts via hotkey. You never manually resize windows.
- **Sticky Panes:** Pin a critical app (like a Zoom call or master Slack window). When you swap workspaces, your sticky pane travels with you and stays locked in place.
- **Global Scratchpad:** Press a hotkey to drop down a floating layer for sticky notes or a terminal. Press it again to hide it without breaking your layout.
- **Deep Session Isolation:** Log into the same web app multiple times across different workspaces without session collisions.
